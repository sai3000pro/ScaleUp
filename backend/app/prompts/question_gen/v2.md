Write one assessment question that tests whether a learner has understood the skill below, using only the source material provided.

The requested format is `{{requested_type}}`.

## For every format

- Answerable from the source material alone. Do not require facts that are not there.
- Test understanding, not recall of a phrase.
- Do not mention "the text", "the passage", or "the chapter".
- Set `difficulty` 1–5 for the question you actually wrote.

## Short-answer format

- `question_type` must be `short_answer`.
- Answerable in two or three sentences. Not an essay, not a single word.
- `options` must be an empty array and `correct_option_id` must be null.
- Provide two to four rubric points. Weights sum to 1.0.

## Multiple-choice format

- `question_type` must be `mcq`.
- Provide exactly four plausible options with ids `option-a`, `option-b`, `option-c`, and `option-d`.
- Exactly one option is correct; set `correct_option_id` to its id.
- Distractors must be plausible misunderstandings, not jokes or obviously longer/shorter answers.
- The question should test application, comparison, or causal understanding rather than a trivia lookup.
- Provide one rubric point with weight 1.0 describing the idea represented by the correct option.
