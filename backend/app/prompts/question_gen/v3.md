Write one source-grounded assessment question for the learner's skill.

Requested format: `{{requested_type}}`.

## Rules for every format

- Use only the supplied source material. Do not require facts that are absent.
- Test understanding or application, not a trivial phrase lookup.
- Do not mention "the text", "the passage", or "the chapter".
- Set `difficulty` from 1 to 5 for the question you actually wrote.
- Return every field in the schema. Use empty arrays and nulls for fields that do not apply.

## short_answer

- `question_type` is `short_answer`.
- Answerable in two or three sentences.
- `options` is `[]`, `correct_option_id` is null, `accepted_answers` is `[]`.
- `code_language` is null and `code_requirements` is `[]`.
- Provide two to four rubric points whose weights sum to 1.0.

## mcq

- `question_type` is `mcq`.
- Provide exactly four plausible options with ids `option-a` through `option-d`.
- Exactly one option is correct in `correct_option_id`.
- `accepted_answers` is `[]`; `code_language` is null; `code_requirements` is `[]`.
- Provide one rubric point with weight 1.0.

## cloze

- `question_type` is `cloze`.
- Put exactly one meaningful blank marker `_____` in the question.
- The blank must be answerable from the source material and test a key term or relationship.
- `options` is `[]` and `correct_option_id` is null.
- Put one to five accepted answers in `accepted_answers`, ordered from most canonical to most natural synonym.
- `code_language` is null and `code_requirements` is `[]`.
- Provide one rubric point with weight 1.0.

## code

- `question_type` is `code`.
- Ask for a small snippet or function grounded in the source material.
- `code_language` is one of `python`, `javascript`, `typescript`, `java`, `sql`, or `pseudocode`.
- `code_requirements` contains one to five short, observable requirements such as `return`, `for`, `dot product`, or `normalize`.
- `options` is `[]`, `correct_option_id` is null, and `accepted_answers` is `[]`.
- Provide one rubric point with weight 1.0.
- The application will perform static requirement matching only. Do not assume execution, imports, filesystem access, network access, or hidden tests.
